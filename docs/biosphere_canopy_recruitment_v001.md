# Biosphere canopy-light recruitment

Live quadstore decoded projection: silver birch and ryegrass have low shade tolerance; pedunculate oak has medium. No ontology values were changed. Explicit `biosphere_growth_profile.light_minimum` takes priority, including zero; otherwise shade categories reuse Species Sim's thresholds (.55/.30/.12). Unknown categories retain growth-form defaults. The picker labels the source.

Woody population envelopes attenuate substrate light through the existing `forest_ecology.light_at` function. Height follows the individual blueprint's height/maturity defaults; opacity combines crown openness and population abundance. Decorative instances never cast ecological shade. Cohort advancement uses a frozen seasonal canopy and 25 area samples, so a shaded centre does not reject bright margins. Juvenile abundance, development, spread, representative sites and satellite establishment respond to light. New adults retain an establishment footprint; pre-existing adult positions are not moved or erased by recruitment filtering.

Fixed 10 × 10 m fixture: two mature oak patches, identical birch/grass seedbeds and candidate coordinates. Recruitment off/on: **1,020/560** fully suitable birch sites; **880/497** display plants. The production renderer's crowns hide the interior, so the comparison includes a top-down light/site diagnostic. Baseline: `artifacts/biosphere_recruitment_v001/baseline.png`; final: `comparison.png` and `results.json`. Reproduce: `py -3.14 -m tools.render_biosphere_recruitment`.

Tests: 105 passed, 28 subtests. Covers tolerance contrasts, zero/unknown/invalid inputs, cache changes, gaps, bright margins, blocked development, establishment holes, determinism and display independence. App capture was unavailable; images are renderer previews, not verified live navigation.

Scientific basis: [Forest Research's silver birch account](https://www.forestresearch.gov.uk/tools-and-resources/tree-species-database/131561-silver-birch-sbi-2/) documents light-demanding pioneer regeneration. The thresholds, opacity, transition width, developmental clock and stress rates remain qualitative defaults. Patch envelopes are not measured crowns; biomass summaries remain patch-level approximations. This does not add nutrient niches, seasonal leaf loss, soil modelling, grazing/fire or colonisation without an introduced species/propagule source.
